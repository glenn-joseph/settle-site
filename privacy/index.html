Settle Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Settle splits a bill between the people at a table. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. If you have questions, contact chronosappsupport@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

The names you enter, the items on your bill and the amounts you split stay on your iPhone. Settle has no accounts and doesn't send this information to us.

Receipt scanning

Receipt scanning is an optional paid feature. It only runs when you choose to scan, and only after you've agreed on the screen shown before your first scan.

When you scan a receipt:

  1. Your photo is sent to our server, which runs on Supabase.
  2. Our server passes it to Anthropic, a third-party AI company, whose AI model Claude reads the amounts and item names.
  3. The result comes back to your iPhone.

We don't store your photo or what was read from it. Our logs record only technical details, such as how many pages were scanned.

Anthropic handles the photo under its commercial terms and privacy policy, and may keep it for a limited time for safety and legal reasons. It doesn't use it to train its models. See anthropic.com/legal.

Receipts can show details such as a restaurant's address, a server's name or the last digits of a card. Only scan receipts you're comfortable sharing.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. Settle gives your device a random, anonymous ID so RevenueCat can confirm your subscription, and our server uses the same ID to count your scans each month. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

Settle uses the camera only while you're scanning a receipt. If you choose a photo instead, it can only see the photos you pick.

Children

Settle isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

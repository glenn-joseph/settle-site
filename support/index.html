Subscriptions

Receipt scanning is an optional subscription billed through your Apple Account. To cancel or change it, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Bought a subscription on another iPhone? Open the scanning screen in Settle and tap Restore.

Receipt scanning

For the best results, photograph the whole receipt flat and in good light. A receipt can be up to 4 pages, and a subscription includes 100 scans a month.
